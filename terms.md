### **8\. Gas Sponsorship (Optional Feature)**

Protofire Multisig may, at Protofire’s sole discretion, offer a feature (“Gas Sponsorship”) whereby Protofire pre-pays the network (gas) fee for an eligible Transaction on your behalf, and you compensate Protofire in a supported Token within the same Transaction. The Tokens supported for this purpose on each Blockchain are indicated in the App at the time you sign the Transaction; Protofire may add, remove or replace supported Tokens for any network at any time.

**How it works.** Where you choose to use Gas Sponsorship, the compensation amount and the Token are specified directly in the Transaction you sign (using the Safe Account's native gasToken and refundReceiver parameters), and network fees are paid from Protofire’s own funds at the point of broadcasting. There is no standing pre-authorization, allowance, or advance deposit - each Transaction is individually signed by you with the exact compensation amount included, and Protofire never takes custody of your funds outside of that single Transaction.

**Availability.** Gas Sponsorship is offered on a discretionary, per-transaction, per-network basis. Protofire may limit, condition, suspend, or discontinue Gas Sponsorship at any time, for any network, transaction type, or User, without notice and without liability.

**Screening.** Transactions using Gas Sponsorship, including the sending and any recipient address(es) involved, are subject to sanctions and other compliance screening. Protofire may decline to sponsor, or may reject, any Transaction for compliance reasons.

**Failed or reverted Transactions.** Compensation is paid as part of the Transaction itself and follows the outcome of that Transaction on the Blockchain, not the success of the underlying operation: (a) if the Transaction is included on the Blockchain but the intended operation does not succeed (e.g., the target contract reverts), the network fee has still been spent and the compensation is not refunded; (b) if the Transaction cannot be included on the Blockchain at all (e.g., insufficient Token balance or an invalid signature), no compensation is charged; (c) if network fees rise between signing and broadcasting beyond the signed amount, we will either broadcast the Transaction within that signed amount or decline to broadcast it and ask you to sign an updated Transaction.

**No guarantee.** Protofire does not guarantee the availability, pricing, or continued offering of Gas Sponsorship. Network fees may fluctuate, and the compensation amount reflects prevailing network conditions and our pricing at the time of the Transaction.

**Your responsibility.** You are responsible for ensuring you hold a sufficient amount of the supported Token to compensate Protofire for any Transaction where you elect to use Gas Sponsorship. Protofire is not responsible for Transactions that fail or are rejected due to insufficient Token balance.

Liability arising from the Gas Sponsorship feature is limited pursuant to Section 19 below.
